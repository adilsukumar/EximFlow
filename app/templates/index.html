The Holy Trinity of DNS Authentication
  • SPF (Sender Policy Framework): Defines authorized IP servers allowed to send mail on behalf of your secondary domain.
  • DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail): 2048-bit cryptographic signature proving the email was not tampered with in transit.
  • DMARC: Strict policy instruction (p=quarantine or p=reject) telling receiving mailboxes how to handle spoofed messages.
  • Custom Tracking Domain: A CNAME mapping (e.g. track.yourdomain.com) to replace generic third-party tracking links with your own SSL-secured domain.
